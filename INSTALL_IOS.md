## 💡 Installation Instructions

### Option 1: AltStore/SideStore (RECOMMENDED)

#### First Time Setup:

1. Install AltStore from [altstore.io](https://altstore.io/) on your computer
2. Install AltStore app on your iPhone (follow AltStore's guide)
3. Sign in with your FREE Apple ID in AltStore

#### Installing Dogechat:

1. Download **dogechat-unsigned.ipa** from the [latest release](https://github.com/GreatApe42069/dogechat/releases/tag/ios-latest-unsigned) Assets ⬇️
2. Open the IPA file with AltStore on your device
3. AltStore will sign it automatically with your Apple ID
4. Tap "Install" and you're done!

#### Auto-Refresh:

- Enable "Background Refresh" in AltStore settings
- AltStore will auto-renew the 7-day signature
- New builds released every 6 days to stay fresh

### Option 2: Jailbroken Devices

The same **dogechat-unsigned.ipa** works perfectly:

1. Download the IPA from the [latest release](https://github.com/GreatApe42069/dogechat/releases/tag/ios-latest-unsigned)
2. Install using Filza, AppSync Unified, or your preferred installer
3. No expiration - works permanently!

### Option 3: Manual Signing (Advanced Users)

1. Download **dogechat-unsigned.app.zip** from the [latest release](https://github.com/GreatApe42069/dogechat/releases/tag/ios-latest-unsigned)
2. Extract the .app bundle
3. Use one of these tools to sign:
   - **iOS App Signer** (Mac) - [Download](https://dantheman827.github.io/ios-app-signer/)
   - **Sideloadly** (Windows/Mac) - [Download](https://sideloadly.io/)
   - **Xcode** (Mac) - Use your developer tools
4. Sign with your own Apple ID credentials
5. Install to your device

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### AltStore won't install the IPA
- Make sure you're signed in with your Apple ID in AltStore
- Check that your device and computer are on the same network
- Try restarting both AltStore on your computer and device

### 7-day expiration
- Enable "Background Refresh" in AltStore settings
- Keep AltStore running on your computer when possible
- The app will auto-refresh signatures in the background

### Installation fails on jailbroken device
- Make sure AppSync Unified is properly installed
- Try using Filza instead if other installers fail
- Check that you have enough storage space
